# Cloud

## Models

### IaaS

Infrastructure as a service.
Biggest customer control over models. Least provider control over models.

Customer controls everything on software end. From OS, to platform and software. Customer has control and responsability over everything besides the physical infrastructure
Provider controls the physical field, from cables to network installation, devices and utilities. Also in charge of the physical security

### SaaS

Software as a service. 
Least customer control over models. Biggest provider control over models. 

The customer does not have control over the software purchased, customer pays for an already build infrastructure, platform and software and has no contro over it, just uses the service.
The provider has controll over all the aspects of the service, from physical infrastructure to OS and platform management and specific software. It also has responsability over the security on all the fields.

## PaaS

Platform as a Service. 

Hybrid model between both IaaS and SaaS

Customer has control over which software it wants working on the platform they pay for. They can choose between different premade setups as per their needs. 

Provider has control over physical infrastructure and also the platform, they provide ready to go environments where the customer can just upload its code or install the software they need. 



## Deployment

### Public cloud

On public cloud the resources are shared among customers. The same hardware can be shared between customers. A same HDD can have A and B data inside of it. The resource management is done by the provider on demand of the customers.
Cheaper, less control and high scalability.

### Private cloud

Resoruces dedicated to a single customer. Similar to renting a whole server for your organization. The whole system is dedicated to a single customer. Better for critical services and data storage. High control, high cost and medium scalability. The hole management is done by the customer besides the physical side of it. 

### Hybrid

Shared environment where private and public cooexist and even communicate or work together. Part of the system is dedicated to the customer for critical parts of data and services while part of the system is shared for less critical use. Medium control, medium cost and high scalability. Also more complex to integrate. Host of sensitive data on a private part of the cloud while the deployment and testing of the service is done on public cloud.
