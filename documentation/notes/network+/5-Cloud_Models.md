# Cloud deployment Models

Public, private, hybrid


## SaaS

On-demand software, no installation or local resources needed. 

Centalized management of data and apps.

## IaaS

On-demand infrastructure

Sometimes Hardware as a service HaaS. Provides de infrastructure to host your own app or service. You data is nott local but you have control over the functionality of the services or apps

Own responsability of management and security

## PaaS

Someone else handles the platform and its security, you manage the development

You can't contol the data, people or infrastructure. Hybrid between SaaS and IaaS
